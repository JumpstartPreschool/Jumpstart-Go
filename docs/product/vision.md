# Vision

## The problem

At Jumpstart Preschool, handing a child over at the gate runs on phone calls and memory:

- the school gets little or no notice that someone is coming;
- when someone other than a parent collects a child, nothing checks that they are the right person;
- once a child leaves with someone else, the parent can't see where they are;
- there is no reliable record of who dropped off or collected each child, or when;
- attendance, notices and parent messages are spread across registers, WhatsApp groups and personal phones;
- there is a rush at the gate while the school hour ends;
- facilitating the flow and making the child ready at the exact time is a challenge;
- announcements of pick-up or drop-off or other activities are mostly through WhatsApp and often get missed;
- there is a lack of communication between the parents and the school regarding the pick-up or drop-off timings.

With children as young as 7 months, every handover has to be verified, recorded and visible.

## What Jumpstart is

A premium, mobile-first platform for Jumpstart families and staff:

- **Parents** request a pickup or drop-off in a few taps and can also set the pick-up/drop-off time for the particular day. They can send someone else if needed, and watch that person's journey live like Uber-style live tracking and get notifications when the child is picked up or dropped off or about the pick-up/drop-off schedule.

- **Coordinators** bring children between the gate and the classrooms, working from their iPads. They get spoken alerts the moment a parent makes a request, see who is arriving next, check the handoff code before releasing a child, and mark attendance. They can also communicate with parents regarding the pick-up/drop-off schedule and get the child ready at the exact time.

- **Core team** can set pick-up and drop-off times for each child inside the programme windows the admin sets, and can also mark them as present or absent or on leave or off.

- **The admin** controls who has access to what, and sees how the whole branch is running at a glance. They add parents and their children to the app and manage their basic information.

## Principles

1. **Safety through verification.** A child is only handed over after a check (the handoff code for a third person, or staff confirming a registered guardian) and a record. GPS supports the process but never replaces it.
2. **Honest data.** Location, ETA and status are always real and show how fresh they are. Stale data is never shown as live.
3. **Calm and premium.** Parents rarely need more than three taps. Staff see the next action without searching for it.
4. **Private by default.** We collect only what we need, only the right people can see it, it is deleted on a schedule, and sensitive actions are logged.
5. **Free-first.** Free and open-source tools until paid tools are clearly needed. Every paid service needs approval.

## Where it starts and where it goes

- **Release 1:** one branch (Karve Nagar, Pune), about 50 children.
- **Later:** more branches, learning progress, a child experience, fees, documents and reports, all built on the same foundation.
