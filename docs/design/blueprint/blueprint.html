Notes, and what the app uses where the website disagrees. The core team can change any of these in the app.

  • 1Saturdays count, except the 2nd and last Saturday of each month.
  • 2Jr KG 360°: the programme page says it starts at 9:45 and the FAQ says 9:00. The app uses 9:00.
  • 3After School: the Karve Road page says 4–7 PM for ages 3–8; another page says 2–6 PM for ages 4–8. The app uses 4–7 PM, so by the rule its pickup is 7:00–7:15, the only pickup after 7 PM (question 7).
  • Your rule: drop-off is the 15 minutes before a class starts, pickup the 15 minutes after it ends. 360°+ is different: the child must be in for the core hours, drop-off is any time before them (from 9:00 for Playgroup and Nursery, 8:45–9:00 for Jr KG and Sr KG) and pickup any time after them until 7 PM. The Learning Center takes children 8:45–9:00 and they can be collected any time until 7 PM.
  • The gate is busiest from 11:30 to 11:45, when morning pickups and afternoon drop-offs happen together, and from 1:00 to 1:15.
  • Not in the app: the Parent Toddler Program (the parent stays for the whole hour), the Early Childhood Expert course for adults, and corporate child care.

What happens to a change

Normal

Inside the child's window, for example pickup 1:00–1:15. Announced to the class's coordinators at once; nothing else to do.

Flagged

Outside the window (with a reason), or less than 15 minutes' notice. Still allowed: a different chime, and a coordinator taps Acknowledge.

Running late

From the parent's +10, +20 or +30, or from the live location. Coordinators hear "running about 10 minutes late".

Not possible

Before 8:45 AM or after 7:15 PM. The app offers Call school instead.

Roles, as you set them

Who can do what

Only two people hold Admin: Ankit Singh and Aditya Tapadia. Nobody else can be given it. The core team plans timings and attendance, and chooses the coordinators and their classes. The PRO team runs the front desk: calls from parents, messages and announcements. Coordinators run the gate on their iPads. Parents, and the family the admin adds (grandparents, aunts, uncles, older siblings), see only their own children and collect without a link. A third person gets one link for one trip. The database checks every one of these rules on every request, not only the screens.

Admin → People · in the Screen Book

Adding a child

  1. The child. Full name, the name used at school, and date of birth. The app shows the age and suggests the class.

  2. Class and programme. First the class (Nursery is suggested for a 3-year-old), then the programme: Half day morning 9:00–11:30, afternoon 11:45–2:15, 360° 11:45–5:30, or 360°+ (in school 11:45–5:30, pickup any time until 7). No separate class group to choose.

  3. Parents. Name, relation, email and mobile, and app access. Parents abroad keep their foreign number, with its country code.

  4. Family who can collect. Grandparents, aunts, uncles and older siblings, chosen from the relation pop-up. They collect from the gate without a link, like parents, and can have the app too. Anyone else is a third person, sent a one-trip link by a parent.

  5. Saved in one step. The child, parents and family are saved together or not at all. Each person with app access signs in with an email code and sees the child's day.

The main safety journey

A pickup, start to finish

Aarav's mother sends the family's driver, Ramesh, to collect him at 1:00, inside his 1:00–1:15 pickup window. Ramesh isn't family the school knows, so he gets a one-trip link and a code. Nobody has to approve the request: the announcement starts the moment she taps, and the school gets Aarav ready while Ramesh is on the way.

Honest location

Every position shows its age: LIVE under 15 seconds, RECENT under a minute, then STALE: the marker stops, turns grey, and the ETA is hidden. UNAVAILABLE means no location, but the handover still works.

ETA as a range

From the real road route (OpenRouteService, working with your key): "about 3–5 min". Never an exact minute, since there's no live traffic data.

A link that can't be misused

One phone (the first to tap Confirm), one trip, and it stops by itself. WhatsApp link previews can't lock it.

Family collect without a link

Grandparents, aunts, uncles and older siblings the admin has added are confirmed from Aarav's list at the gate, exactly like his parents.

Privacy after handover

Once Aarav is handed over, only his parents see the trip home. The school no longer sees the location.

On the coordinators' iPads

How announcements reach the right iPad

Built

Choosing the coordinators

  1. The core team (or the admin) opens Coordinators. Each class lists its coordinators. A class with nobody is marked in orange, because its requests go to every coordinator on duty.

  2. Add coordinator: choose someone the admin has already added, then tick their classes. If that person is also a parent here, the app warns that they will see every child's pickup details.

  3. Edit changes a coordinator's classes. Remove takes away the Coordinator role, and their classes with it; any other roles stay.

  4. The database enforces the limits. The core team can give only the Coordinator role, so nobody can raise their own access. Every change goes into the audit log.

One iPad limit to know about. A web app can only speak while it's open on screen. Start duty keeps the screen awake and checks the speaker, and a notification reaches a locked iPad as a backup. If the school ever needs it to speak while locked, that takes a native iPad app ($99 a year from Apple, only with your approval).

Still to decide

Questions for you

These affect how the gate works on a real day. Each has my suggestion, so a quick "yes" is enough, or tell me what the school does instead.

Question 1 · At the gate

Someone who isn't on the list comes for a child.

No link, not a guardian: an uncle, or a neighbour asked at the last minute.

I suggest

The child is not released. The coordinator taps Call parent in the app, and the parent can send that person a link on the spot, which gives them a code. The attempt is recorded.

Question 2 · Safety

Should the admin be able to mark a person who must not collect a child?

For example, a custody arrangement or a court order.

I suggest

Yes. Only the admin can add it; coordinators see it in red at the gate. Parents don't see the note, so it can't cause conflict at home.

Question 3 · Two guardians

Do both parents have the same say?

Changing today's time, sending a third person, reporting an absence.

I suggest

Yes, equally, and the other parent is told about every change the moment it's made.

Question 4 · Siblings

One adult collects two children from different classes.

For example, Jr KG and the Learning Center at the same time.

I suggest

One request covers both children. Each class's coordinators hear their own child, and each handover is confirmed separately.

Question 5 · Closing time

Nobody has come by 7:15 PM.

The school closes, but the child is still there.

I suggest

At 7:00 PM the app alerts the core team, the parents and the family; at 7:15 it asks the core team to call. Please tell me who stays with the child, so the app can name them.

Question 6 · Age

Is there a minimum age for the adult collecting?

For example, an older brother or sister of 15 or 16.

I suggest

18 or older for anyone sent as a third person, and for an older brother or sister added as family. The admin confirms the age when adding them.

Question 7 · After School

After School ends at 7:00 PM. Is pickup 7:00 to 7:15?

That follows your 15-minute rule, but it is the only pickup after the school day ends.

I suggest

Keep 7:00–7:15 for After School only, and say so on the parents' screen.

Question 8 · Learning Center

Do Learning Center children only arrive 8:45 to 9:00?

You said drop-off 8:45–9:00 and pickup any time until 7. Some daycare children may come later, for example after a morning class elsewhere.

I suggest

Keep 8:45–9:00 as the window. A later drop-off is still allowed with a reason, and the coordinator sees it flagged.
