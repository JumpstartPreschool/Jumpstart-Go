Foundations

Calm surfaces, one clear next step

A bright pearl canvas, deep ink text, and white cards. Frosted glass only where something floats: the top bar, the tab bar and the announcement bar. The next action is always Jumpstart blue.

Logo placement

The logo is the most playful thing on screen, so everything around it stays quiet. It never sits on a photo, never stretches, and always keeps clear space equal to the height of its stars.

Sign in: centred, large

56–64 px tall on a phone, with "Preschool & Learning Center · Karve Road" underneath. The first thing a parent sees.

Admin

Inside the app: top left

36–40 px in the top bar, followed by the area's chip (Admin, Core team). The account button sits on the right.

On duty12:10

iPad on duty: left, with the clock

Small and steady, so the clock and the announcement bar own the screen.

Home Screen: the smiling star

The star alone on a white tile, until your own icon design is ready. The wordmark is too wide to read at icon size.

Buttons

Solid colour sitting on a darker lip, like a real key. Hover deepens the colour. Press sinks the button onto its lip. No gradients, no glows, no shine. One main button per screen, always Jumpstart blue, and every target at least 44 points tall.

  • Face #1560D4, white label in Jakarta 750, 16–17 px
  • Lip 4 px of #0C3E8F under the face: it's what makes it pressable
  • Corners 16 px: friendly, not a pill
  • Press moves down 3 px and the lip shrinks to 1 px, in 60 ms

Motion

Things move like they have weight: a quick press, a soft spring when a sheet or a card arrives. Motion always explains a change and never blocks it. With Reduce Motion on, every change still happens, just without the movement.

Coordinator · iPad

Coordinator pages

For an iPad carried between the gate and the classrooms: big targets, a clock you can read across the room, and announcements that repeat until someone answers.

Ready for the gate?

Start your duty and this iPad announces every pickup and drop-off for your classes, and keeps its screen on until you finish.

  • Opened from the Home Screen

    Alerts reach a locked iPad as a backup.

  • The screen stays on

    While you're on duty, it won't sleep.

  • Sound check

    A short chime plays when you start.

  • Connected

    Requests arrive the moment a parent sends them.

  • Starting duty unlocks sound (iPads only allow it after a tap) and keeps the screen awake.
C2

Gate queue and hand over

iPad · coordinator · live at the top
  • The iPad in the demo at the top: Arriving next, Coming up and Done today, with the announcement bar always visible.
  • The one button is always the next step: Acknowledge, then Ready at the gate, then Hand over.
  • Each iPad announces its own classes first. If nobody answers in 2 minutes, every coordinator on duty hears it.
  • Hand over lists only the child's registered guardians. A third person needs the 4-digit code instead.
