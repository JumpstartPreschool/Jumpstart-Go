Foundations

Calm surfaces, one clear next step

A pearl canvas with soft rainbow light, deep ink text, and frosted glass only where something floats: the top bar, the tab bar, sheets and the announcement bar. The next action is always Jumpstart blue.

Logo placement

The logo is the most playful thing on screen, so everything around it stays quiet. It never sits on a photo, never stretches, and always keeps clear space equal to the height of its stars.

Sign in: centred, large

56–64 px tall on a phone, with "Preschool & Learning Center · Karve Road" underneath. The first thing a parent sees.

Admin

Inside the app: top left

36–40 px in the glass top bar, followed by the area's colour chip (Admin, Core team). The account button sits on the right.

On duty12:10

iPad on duty: left, with the clock

Small and steady, so the clock and the announcement bar own the screen. Hidden on phones to leave room for the queue.

Home Screen: the smiling star

The star alone on a warm white tile, until your own icon design is ready. The wordmark is too wide to read at icon size.

Motion

Things move like they have weight: a quick squeeze on press and a soft spring on release. Motion always explains a change and never blocks it. With Reduce Motion on, every change still happens, just without the movement.

Coordinator pages

Designed for an iPad carried between the gate and the classrooms: huge targets, a clock you can read from across the room, and announcements that repeat until someone answers.

Ready for the gate?

Start your duty and this iPad announces every pickup and drop-off for your classes, and keeps its screen on until you finish.

  • Opened from the Home Screen

    Alerts reach a locked iPad as a backup.

  • The screen stays on

    While you're on duty, it won't sleep.

  • Sound check

    A short chime plays when you start.

  • Connected

    Requests arrive the moment a parent sends them.

Gate queue

iPad · coordinator · live above
  • This is the iPad in the demo at the top: Arriving next, Coming up and Done today, with the announcement bar always visible.
  • The row's one button is always the next step: Acknowledge, then Ready at the gate, then Hand over.
  • Each iPad announces its own classes first. If nobody answers in 2 minutes, every coordinator on duty hears it.
  • Hand over lists only the child's registered guardians. A third person needs the 4-digit code instead.
